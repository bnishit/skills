# Manuscript evaluation rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5 and cite passages or chapter-level evidence. A low score is a diagnosis, not an instruction to average away the book's personality.

## Shared dimensions

1. **Promise:** The manuscript delivers the experience established at the start.
2. **Architecture:** Chapters have distinct jobs and create cumulative movement.
3. **Middle:** Pressure, inquiry, or discovery develops rather than stalls or repeats.
4. **Specificity:** Concrete scenes, examples, or arguments replace generic claims.
5. **Voice:** The prose remains coherent and recognizably belongs to this author or narrator.
6. **Reader orientation:** Necessary context arrives when useful without explaining what the reader already understands.
7. **Ending:** The close earns its meaning from the manuscript and fulfills rather than summarizes it.
8. **Integrity:** Privacy, evidence, canon, attribution, and stated boundaries are respected.

## Mode dimensions

### Personal or reflective

- Emotional claims are supported by lived moments or honest reasoning.
- Uncertainty and contradiction remain human rather than being polished away.
- The intended reader is addressed without pressure, self-defense, or sacrifice accounting.

### Nonfiction

- Consequential claims match their evidence and reveal material limitations.
- Counterarguments are engaged in their strongest useful form.
- The reader can distinguish fact, inference, example, and recommendation.

### Fiction

- Plot turns arise mainly from character choices and prior consequences.
- Character change is visible through decisions under increasing cost.
- Timeline, knowledge, world rules, clues, and point of view stay coherent.
- The premise's special mechanism intensifies the human story rather than replacing it.

## Gates before lock

Do not lock while any of these remain:

- a chapter has no unique job;
- a central claim lacks a source or an explicit uncertainty label;
- a major plot turn depends on missing causality;
- a real person's motive is presented as fact without support;
- named sensitive content violates an author boundary;
- late changes have not been propagated through the ledgers and ending.

End an evaluation with the three highest-leverage revisions, in order. Do not provide dozens of equal-weight notes.
