## Worked hypothesis, not a finding

For long-distance couples who get stuck replaying one competitive game, a bounded number of rounds may help them move to other kinds of shared play and leave a reason to return tomorrow. This depends on both partners welcoming the transition. If they instead stop disappointed, or one partner returns mainly out of obligation, the proposed benefit is in doubt.

The recorded evidence contains personal reports and reasoning. It does not establish retention lift, prevalence across couples, the founders' intent, or whether two rounds are better than one. The once-per-day rule was discussed as the user's observation; the captured assistant explicitly said it had not found that rule in the article.
